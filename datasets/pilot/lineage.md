# Lineage

All cases were authored on 2026-09-13 for synthetic calibration. Source images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then copied into `assets/` without programmatic image editing. Generation prompts and planned-vs-observed notes are in `manifest.json`.

The four source-image groups are intentionally isolated in one development split. No case is a final holdout and no real robot frame was available. Synthetic exhibit IDs (`tour_demo`, `exhibit_mechanism`, `exhibit_vessel`, `exhibit_sculpture`) and supplied facts are fictional scenario context.
